How we work

This is a small practice on purpose. The structure below is how engagements actually run.

2 to 3 clients at a time

We take on 2 to 3 clients at a time. That focus is the point: your job gets real attention, not a rotating cast.

2 to 3 month engagements

Engagements typically run 2 to 3 months. Long enough to fix what is broken and make the fix stick, short enough to stay sharp.

Step down, don't stop

When the intensive work is done, most clients step down to ongoing support at a lower monthly investment, sized to what the site still needs. Support continues as long as it is useful, and stops when it isn't.

Entry points

Four ways in. Each one ends with something you can use.

All prices and resource levels on this page are starting points. The exact investment from each side, along with scope and timing, is confirmed case by case in the terms and retainer agreement, as defined and agreed before work starts.

Site Performance Audit

Best when something on site feels off and you want facts before you spend more.

$2,500 flat

One fixed price, confirmed on the call.

  • Full-day on-site walkthrough
  • Field-Level Insight Report: what is working, what is broken, what needs attention
  • Site-Specific Action Plan: tailored steps for your job, team, and phase
  • 2-week action sketch and one bottleneck to unblock
  • Report and plan turned around in 48 to 72 hours, subject to the terms and retainer agreement

Project Readiness Pack

Best when a job is about to start and you want day one clean.

Starting at $3,500

Final quote follows the call, the site, and the scope.

  • Permits, locates, access, utilities, and traffic squared away before day one
  • Two-week lookahead and procurement: materials, subs, permits, and access lined up
  • Task planning sheets and crew briefs: method, hazards, interfaces, and what done looks like
  • Handoff checklist the crew can actually follow
  • Advance notice of the proceed date is needed for best results: the earlier we know day one, the more we can have squared away before it starts

Embedded Site Leadership

Best when you need a steady hand on site through start-up, shutdown, handover, or a gap in supervision.

$1,500 per day

Multi-week engagements quoted per engagement.

  • Daily prep, tailboards, and field planning support
  • Crew alignment and accountability reset
  • Gap cover for absent supers or overloaded teams
  • Conflict management with subs or internal crews
  • Typically 2 to 3 months, then steps down to ongoing support

Ongoing Support

Best when the intensive work is done and you want to keep the gains.

$1,500 per month

Month to month. The level can step down further as the site steadies.

  • A typical month: one site walk, a weekly rhythm check, and on-call help during business hours
  • On-call help for planning, permits, client communication, and incidents
  • Foreperson and lead-hand coaching
  • Safety and culture routines that stick
  • Guardrails: this does not include full-time site presence or extra site days, which are quoted separately. Exact hours, response times, and scope are set in the terms and retainer agreement.

Also available inside any engagement

Culture and communication work (tone coaching, reset sessions, incentive design), changed conditions support (flagged early, with clear facts on scope, time, and cost), safety documentation tune-ups, and incident investigation. If it fits the job, it fits the engagement.
