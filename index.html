Project Readiness in detail

Project Readiness has two stages: an independent review, followed by optional implementation.

Stage A: Find the gaps

$3,500 standard / $5,000 complex

Stage A Readiness Review. Fixed price, desktop-based, five business days from receipt of the agreed documents. A document-led review of one construction phase or work package. We examine the plan, identify execution risks, and provide practical recommendations before work starts.

  • Stage A, Readiness Review: an independent, primarily document-based review of one agreed construction phase or complete work package
  • Sequencing, labour and equipment readiness, site logistics, contractor interfaces, permits, and known constraints
  • Deliverables: Field-Level Insight Report, Five Priority Execution Risks ranked by potential impact, Site-Specific Action Plan with prioritized recommendations, and one findings-review meeting

What we need from you: scope or drawings, schedule or sequence, access and logistics plan, labour, equipment and subcontractor plan, and known constraints, permits and interfaces. Missing items are documented, not assumed.

Standard vs complex: Standard means one phase or work package, up to about 10 key documents, and no more than two significant contractor interfaces. Complex means more than that. We confirm which in writing before work starts.

Scope and requirements
  • Minimum starting documents: scope of work and applicable construction drawings, current schedule or proposed construction sequence, site logistics, access, and staging information, resource and subcontractor plans, known outstanding constraints, permits, and interfaces
  • Missing documents are identified and their effect on the review agreed before commencement
  • Does not include: engineering certification, formal regulatory compliance approval, direct construction supervision, detailed schedule production, or physical site inspection

Stage B: Address the gaps, optional

Quoted from $3,500

Optional practical support to implement agreed readiness recommendations. Stage B is optional, separately scoped and priced. You can take the Stage A findings and implement them with your own team.

  • Permit, locate, utility, access, and traffic coordination
  • Two-week lookaheads and procurement planning
  • Task planning sheets and crew briefs
  • Contractor interface coordination
  • Readiness tracking and handoff checklists
  • Scope, duration, resource commitments, and deliverables are agreed separately

Credit for Continuing Work

How the credit works

When an assessment leads into related work, you should not have to pay twice for the same assessment. If you agree in writing to the next engagement within 30 calendar days after we issue the written proposal for that next engagement, we apply a credit against the next fee, subject to the conditions below. The credit is up to 50% of the fee you paid, and never more than 50% of the next engagement's fee. The credit applies to the same site, construction phase, or work package, to one engagement only, and is confirmed in your written proposal.

One credit per new engagement. Credits cannot be combined, transferred, redeemed for cash, or carried forward. The original service must be completed and paid in full. All figures are CAD before HST.

Examples

  • Site Performance Audit to Project Readiness Review: $2,500 audit to $3,500 review, credit $1,250, payable $2,250.
  • Project Readiness Review to Implementation: $3,500 review to $5,000 implementation, credit $1,750, payable $3,250.
  • Project Readiness Review to Implementation: $5,000 complex review to a $3,500 implementation, credit $1,750, payable $1,750.
  • Audit or Review to Ongoing Support: credit toward the first month's $1,500 retainer only, at most $750 under the 50% cap, no roll into month two.
